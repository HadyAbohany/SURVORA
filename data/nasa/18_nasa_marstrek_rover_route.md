## Mars Trek: elevation, slope and rover routes

Mars Trek overlays maps from spacecraft instruments. The Mars Orbiter Laser Altimeter, or MOLA, measured surface heights using a laser. Height is how high a place is, while slope tells how quickly height changes along the ground. Rover route planners can compare distances and terrain on different map layers. A short path is not automatically the easiest path, and a global map may miss obstacles visible to a rover.
