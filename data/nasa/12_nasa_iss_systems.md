## Electricity and station life support

The International Space Station uses solar arrays to turn sunlight into electricity. That electricity supports research and daily station operations. Its life support equipment supplies oxygen, removes carbon dioxide from cabin air, and recycles wastewater. These are different jobs. The station design does not determine how SURVORA allocates power.
