## Microgravity, muscles and bones

In the near weightlessness of an orbiting station, bones and muscles do less work supporting the body. Astronauts exercise with equipment that supplies resistance to help maintain muscle and bone. Mars has gravity, so what happens aboard the ISS cannot be used as a direct measurement of health changes on Mars.
