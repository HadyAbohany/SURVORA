## Water recovery

On the International Space Station, equipment collects cabin moisture from breath and sweat. A processor also recovers water from urine. The combined system purifies reclaimed water and checks its quality. The brine processor demonstrated additional recovery from the liquid left over by the urine processor. This was a station demonstration, not a recovery rate for Mars or for the game.
