# Power, dust and batteries

These notes explain concepts for children aged 7–12. Do not treat them as measured Mars base readings or as a replacement for full scientific sources.

## Why does a dust storm affect solar panels?

Dust suspended in the air can block sunlight, and dust deposited on panels can reduce the light reaching solar cells.

## Is a Martian dust storm the same as a solar storm?

No. A Martian dust storm is a weather event involving airborne dust. A solar storm involves activity from the Sun and possible space-radiation hazards.

## Why does the base need batteries?

Solar output varies with daylight, shading and dust. Stored energy helps keep crucial systems operating when production is low.

## Should the player always shut down the greenhouse?

Not automatically. The right choice depends on reserve energy, oxygen, crew safety, crop needs, and how long the event lasts. The game engine provides those values.

## Can wind clean panels?

Dust-clearing winds have helped some Mars rovers, but such events are unpredictable; a mission should not rely on them.
