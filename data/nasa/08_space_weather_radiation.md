# Space weather and radiation

These notes explain concepts for children aged 7–12. Do not treat them as measured Mars base readings or as a replacement for full scientific sources.

## What is a solar flare?

A solar flare is a burst of electromagnetic energy associated with activity on the Sun.

## What is a coronal mass ejection?

A CME is a large release of plasma and magnetic field from the Sun. Its effects depend on direction and the environment encountered.

## What are solar energetic particles?

High-energy particles from solar activity can create a radiation hazard for astronauts and spacecraft.

## Is every solar flare dangerous to astronauts on Mars?

No. A flare record alone cannot determine the radiation dose on the Martian surface. Location, timing, magnetic and particle conditions, and shielding matter.

## Why use a radiation shelter?

Shielding and operational precautions can help reduce exposure. The best response depends on the event and available protection.

## Can NASA DONKI predict our game base radiation dose?

Not directly. DONKI records and analyzes space-weather events; converting those events into a local surface dose would require additional scientific models and assumptions.
