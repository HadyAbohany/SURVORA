# Terrain and maps

These notes explain concepts for children aged 7–12. Do not treat them as measured Mars base readings or as a replacement for full scientific sources.

## What does Mars Trek show?

Mars Trek provides maps and geospatial datasets, including imagery and elevation products that can help create plausible Martian terrain.

## Why is a steep rover route harder?

Slopes and obstacles can affect traction, stability, driving time and energy use. Exact energy values are calculated by the game, not directly from a terrain image.

## Does a map tell us where every water deposit is?

No. Geographic imagery and elevation alone cannot establish subsurface water at each spot. Claims require relevant scientific observations.
