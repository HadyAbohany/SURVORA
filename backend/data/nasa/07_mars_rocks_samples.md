# Mars rocks and samples

These notes explain concepts for children aged 7–12. Do not treat them as measured Mars base readings or as a replacement for full scientific sources.

## What is an igneous rock?

An igneous rock forms when molten material cools and solidifies. These rocks can help study volcanic or impact-related history.

## What is a sedimentary rock?

Sedimentary rocks form as particles or other material accumulate and become rock. They can preserve clues to past environments, including water.

## What is regolith?

Regolith refers to loose material covering solid rock, such as dust, sand and broken rock fragments.

## What is special about carbonates?

Carbonate minerals can record interactions between rocks, water and chemistry; they are useful scientific targets, not proof of life.

## Did Perseverance collect real samples?

Yes. NASA maintains a catalog of rock cores, regolith and other samples, with names, types and mission metadata.
