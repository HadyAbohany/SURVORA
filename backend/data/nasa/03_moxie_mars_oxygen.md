# MOXIE science

These notes explain concepts for children aged 7–12. Do not treat them as measured Mars base readings or as a replacement for full scientific sources.

## Did NASA make oxygen on Mars?

Yes. MOXIE on Perseverance operated 16 times, producing 122 grams of oxygen in total. In its best run rate it produced up to 12 grams per hour.

## Could MOXIE support a whole colony?

No. It was a small experimental device. A future settlement would need larger systems, storage, maintenance, and redundancy.

## Why use carbon dioxide?

The atmosphere of Mars is rich in carbon dioxide. MOXIE used an electrochemical process to separate oxygen from carbon dioxide.

## Does finding oxygen in Mars air mean we can breathe it?

No. The oxygen concentration and air pressure are not suitable for breathing. A suit or safe habitat remains necessary.
