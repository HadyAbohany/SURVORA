# Radiation measurements context

These notes explain concepts for children aged 7–12. Do not treat them as measured Mars base readings or as a replacement for full scientific sources.

## What does RadLab contain?

RadLab offers radiation-dosimetry measurements from instruments aboard spacecraft such as the ISS, along with metadata and units.

## What are absorbed dose and dose equivalent?

Absorbed dose describes energy deposited per unit mass. Dose equivalent additionally reflects radiation quality for biological relevance; they are not interchangeable.

## Is an ISS radiation reading the same as a Mars surface reading?

No. The station and Mars have different radiation environments and shielding. Do not pass ISS dose rates off as measurements taken at the SURVORA base.
