## Radiation measurements and instruments

NASA RadLab lets researchers query radiation measurements by timestamp, spacecraft and instrument. For example, a DosTel absorbed dose rate aboard the ISS measures energy deposited per unit mass per unit time at the instrument. It describes that orbital environment and instrument location. It is not a measurement on the Mars surface, an astronaut health limit, or the current radiation in a game shelter.
