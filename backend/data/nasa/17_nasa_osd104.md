## Mouse muscle in an orbital research study

OSD-104 is a NASA Open Science Data Repository study of soleus muscle from mice in the Rodent Research-1 investigation. The soleus is a muscle used to help support the body and move the ankle. The archive records measurements of gene activity and related biological changes. This study can help researchers ask how muscle responds to spaceflight. It does not establish how much muscle a person would lose on Mars.
