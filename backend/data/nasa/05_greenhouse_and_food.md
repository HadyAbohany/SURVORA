# Space agriculture and food

These notes explain concepts for children aged 7–12. Do not treat them as measured Mars base readings or as a replacement for full scientific sources.

## Why do plants need lights in a greenhouse?

Plants need useful light for photosynthesis. NASA has grown plants using carefully controlled LED lighting in space.

## What do plants need besides light?

Water, nutrients, appropriate temperatures, gas exchange, root oxygen, and healthy growth conditions all matter.

## Why can too much water be harmful?

Roots need access to oxygen; waterlogging can disrupt the balance of water and air around roots.

## Can astronauts grow all of their food today?

Current space plant systems demonstrate and investigate food cultivation, but they do not supply a complete diet for an entire crew.

## What is Veggie?

Veggie is a plant-growing system tested aboard the International Space Station. It uses plant pillows with growth media and lighting.

## Why is food a resource-management choice?

Growing food consumes water, power, space and maintenance time; it may supplement stored provisions and improve long missions.
