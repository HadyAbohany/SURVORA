## Space weather observations and forecasts

The NASA CCMC DONKI catalog connects solar flare, coronal mass ejection (CME), and solar energetic particle (SEP) records. It includes observations, analyses, model results and notifications. A flare, an expanding cloud of solar material, and a particle event are different kinds of records. A timestamp describes the cataloged event or observation, not a Mars arrival time or a local radiation dose.
